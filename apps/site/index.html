Questions

Something else? Open an issue on GitHub.

Is it really free?→

Yes. Bouvio is open source under MIT. You pay your model provider directly, or nothing at all with local models.

Where does my code go?→

It stays on the machines you choose. Agents run on your computer or your server, and your keys stay in your keychain.

Do I need a server?→

Not on your own. For a team, one small VPS runs the hub that keeps everyone in sync.

Can I use my Claude subscription?→

Yes. Bouvio drives Claude Code and Codex the same way you do, with your own login.

Can I add my own features?→

Plugins can add pages, panels, commands and settings. The built-in areas use the same system.

Free, open source and yours

Your keys stay in your keychain. Your hub runs on your own server.
